The problem was never information. It is a brain over 200,000 years old running in markets it was never designed to understand.

We are not financial education. We are a forensic analysis of behavior: documentary-grade essays that name the mechanism, trace it to the research, and show where it operates in your own life.

IV. The Standard

Every claim has an address. Go and check it.

Most money content asks you to trust the narrator. We ask you to do the opposite. Each study, year, sample size and effect we cite is real, named and traceable to a peer-reviewed journal, a classic text or an official source, and the full reference sits in the description of every video.

Our one ruleWe never fabricate a number, a quote or a researcher. If a finding is contested or has been challenged, we say so on screen.
01

Verifiable by design

Every claim traces to a peer-reviewed paper, a classic behavioral-economics text or an official source. Nothing rests on "experts say".

02

Authority in 29 words

A concrete, verifiable number appears before the 29th word of every opening, so abstraction never gets the chance to win.

03

Geographic precision

A national statistic names its country and its source. A local finding is never inflated into a law of all humanity.

04

Mechanism, named

No jargon without translation. Each concept is decoded into the decision you recognize from your own life, then tied back to the evidence.
